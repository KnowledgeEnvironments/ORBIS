before. Write one summary to replace all of it, for whoever carries the conversation on.

Keep: who is speaking and what they call each other, names, decisions, facts stated, paths, commands
and their outcomes, questions still open, what was promised. Keep exact words where they matter.

sentences, past tense, at most a page. Keep a reference as it stands, `[[a1|name]]`, where you name
