Every agent carries one mark, for you: what, if anything, it wants from you.

- **`!` needs you:** an approval card waits on you, or it sent a loud notification you have not read.
- **Cross, failed:** it stalled on work you asked for, and you have not seen that.
- **Tick, finished:** it said something to you that you have not seen.
- **Tape, working:** its loop is running.
- **Dot, idle:** nothing new.
- **Hollow dot, inactive:** it has never been woken.

A mark shows on the object list, on chat tabs and on the map, and the bottom line of the window adds them up. Click that line to go to the next one.

A mark clears when you open that agent's chat with the window in front of you, not when the agent finishes. What you have seen is `view/seen` on your own Principal, so it follows you to every client, and only your own session can write it. An agent reading a result does not count as you seeing it.

A mark is for work that is for you. A subagent answering its parent says nothing to you, so ten agents passing work around give you one mark, from the agent that answers you. A `!` always shows, whichever agent raised it.
