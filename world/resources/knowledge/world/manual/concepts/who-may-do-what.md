# Who may do what

A Grant reaches an Object: read, write, evaluate, transmit, create, delegate. A new Principal gets a
Grant strictly narrower than its creator's, and it cannot delegate. A miss and a refusal are the same
answer, `unresolved`.

Some things no Principal decides, root included: `policy/*`, approving a card, unsafe mode and leaving
the box belong to a person's own session. An op in `policy/approval` waits for a person's card unless a
standing rule admits it. Text in a Message carries no authority.

Shells run in a box: they cannot read the world's data or write outside their root.

See the Authority book for the whole of it.
