# What this is

ORBIS is the On-line Relative Basis Information System: a shared knowledge environment for doing work
with people, computers, and artificial intelligence. For the engineers: Objects, Routes, Bindings,
Instructions, Serialization.

One world, one record. The world is a process whose append-only log is the truth; everything else is
a fold of that log. People, agents, models, shells and folders are all Objects in it, and Fields hold
what is known about them.

- An Object is anything with an id. A Field is a typed value on an Object, with a history.
- A Principal can act: a person, or an agent with a loop and a model.
- A Route joins Principals. A Message goes over a Route, and it wakes the Principal it reaches.
- A Grant says what a Principal may reach. Every access goes through one check, resolution.
- An Adapter is equipment: a mounted folder, or a shell.
- A wake is one Principal answering one Message, in up to a few turns, each a model call.
- A Task is a record of work, such as a command that ran or a card a person decided.

The prompt is rebuilt from Fields on every wake. Nothing is remembered that is not on the record.
